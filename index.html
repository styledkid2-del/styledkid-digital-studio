Starting something new should feel clear, not complicated.

Do I need to know exactly what I want? +

No. Tell us what your business does and what feels difficult right now. We can help you choose a sensible first step before you commit to a package.

Are the prices fixed? +

The prices shown are starting estimates in Kenyan shillings. The final quote depends on the agreed work, and you can review it before deciding whether to proceed.

Will you manage my social media or website every month? +

The packages describe starter projects, not an automatic monthly retainer. Any ongoing support, posting schedule or maintenance would need to be discussed and agreed separately.

Does this form send my enquiry to you? +

FormSubmit confirms when it accepts your submission, but that does not guarantee its notification email reached the studio. We aim to reply by email within 2 business days. If you do not hear back, contact us directly by WhatsApp.
