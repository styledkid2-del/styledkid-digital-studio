What the form does

When you submit the enquiry form, the website sends the form fields to FormSubmit, a third-party form delivery service, for email forwarding to doreennkonge81@gmail.com. The recipient must activate the service through its confirmation email before normal delivery can be relied on. FormSubmit processes the submitted information under its own terms and privacy practices. The site does not store submissions in a Kioo Studio database.

The form also prepares a copy of your enquiry for WhatsApp. If you choose “Send on WhatsApp,” your browser opens WhatsApp with the message prefilled for 0740354169 (+254 740 354169). You must review and press Send yourself. WhatsApp then processes the message under its own privacy practices. Copy, device sharing (if supported), and text-file download are also available.

What you choose to share

Please share only information needed to discuss your business enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details, identity document numbers or other sensitive information. The email address and WhatsApp number shown here are the contact destinations configured for this website.

Third-party services

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's servers to retrieve those fonts. The website is currently hosted on Vercel, which may process technical information needed to deliver the site.

Before the studio launches

The business name, contact details, service terms and data-handling practices must be confirmed before accepting customers. Email delivery must be tested after the receiving inbox completes FormSubmit activation. The WhatsApp button only opens a prepared message; it cannot send without the visitor’s action.
