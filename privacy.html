This is a concept website for Kioo Studio. Please read this notice before using the enquiry form.

What the form does

The enquiry form prepares a message in your browser. You may copy the message, download it as a text file, or use your device’s share options if supported. The website does not submit it to a server, save it in a database, or send it to Kioo Studio. You choose whether and where to share the message.

What you choose to share

Please share only information needed to discuss your business enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details, identity document numbers or other sensitive information.

Third-party services

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser may contact Google's servers to retrieve those fonts. The website is currently hosted on Vercel, which may process technical information needed to deliver the site.

Before the studio launches

The business name, contact details, service terms and any future data-handling practices must be confirmed before accepting customers. If a server-based form, analytics, booking tool or payment service is added, this notice must be updated to explain what it collects and why.

This notice is an initial transparency statement, not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy.
