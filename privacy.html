Your information.
Your choice.

This notice explains how the Kioo Studio website handles information shared through its enquiry form. Please read it before submitting your details.

What the form does

When you submit the enquiry form, the name, email address and details you enter are sent to FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service, using the endpoint configured by Kioo Studio. FormSubmit processes the submission and may send a notification email according to the account settings. A successful submission confirms that FormSubmit accepted the form, not that its notification email reached the studio. The studio should monitor submissions and confirm delivery. We aim to reply by email within 2 business days; if you have not heard back, please contact us by WhatsApp. Do not submit passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information.

If the form cannot be submitted, the email fallback opens your email app with the message addressed to doreennkonge81@gmail.com. You must review and press Send yourself; this option depends on an email app being configured on your device.

If the form cannot be submitted, a WhatsApp fallback opens WhatsApp with the message prefilled for +254 740 354169. You must review and press Send yourself. WhatsApp then processes the message under its own privacy practices. Copy and text-file download are also available as backups.

What you choose to share

Please share only information needed to discuss your business enquiry. Do not include passwords, payment details, identity document numbers or other sensitive information. The email address and WhatsApp number shown here are the contact destinations configured for this website.

Third-party services

FormSubmit processes submissions made through the enquiry form. Its own privacy terms and service practices apply to information it receives. The site also loads web fonts from Google Fonts, and is hosted on Vercel; those services may process technical information needed to deliver the site.

Website analytics

The site includes tracking code for Vercel Web Analytics to understand which pages are visited and which enquiry actions are used, such as clicking a contact link, opening WhatsApp, selecting a service or successfully submitting the form. Analytics event data is limited to the action and a short label, selected goal, budget range or suggested service; it does not include enquiry message text, names or email addresses. Vercel may process technical information such as page URL, referrer, device/browser details and approximate location as described in its own privacy documentation. Analytics collection becomes active only when Web Analytics is enabled for the website’s Vercel project. Until then, these events are not collected.

For information about Vercel’s data practices, please review Vercel’s privacy notice. If you prefer not to use the enquiry form, you can contact the studio directly by email or WhatsApp.

Keeping this notice current

FormSubmit notification settings and delivery should be checked regularly. If the form cannot be submitted, you can contact the studio by email or WhatsApp, then review and press Send in the app you choose. This notice should be updated whenever the site’s services or data-handling practices change.

This notice is an initial transparency statement, not legal advice or a substitute for a reviewed privacy policy.
