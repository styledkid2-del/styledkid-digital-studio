Cookies & similar tech.
What this site uses.

This page explains the current design of the Kioo Studio website. It should be read together with our Privacy Notice, which explains enquiry forms, third-party services and analytics more broadly.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small pieces of information a website may ask your browser to store. Similar technologies include local storage, pixels and scripts that measure site use. Some are essential to a service; others remember preferences, measure traffic or support advertising.

What our website currently does

The site displays a consent panel for optional analytics. Analytics is off unless a visitor chooses “Accept analytics”; choosing “Reject optional” keeps analytics disabled. The choice is stored in browser local storage so the panel does not appear on every page view. A “Cookie settings” control remains available to change the choice later. If browser storage is unavailable, the choice applies only for the current page view.

The website includes a Vercel Web Analytics script and event-tracking calls. Vercel states that its Web Analytics product does not use cookies; it uses a short-lived, request-derived hash for visitor statistics instead. Whether analytics data is actually collected depends on the Vercel project’s analytics configuration. See Vercel’s analytics privacy documentation.

Third-party services and links

The site requests fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google to retrieve font files. If you submit the enquiry form, FormSubmit processes that submission. If you choose WhatsApp or an external social link, your browser connects to that provider. Those providers operate their own services and privacy practices, which may include technologies outside Kioo Studio’s control.

This page does not claim that third-party services never use cookies or other identifiers on their own websites. Their practices can vary by service, browser and account settings.

Your choices

You can choose “Reject optional” in the consent panel, or reopen “Cookie settings” at the lower-left of the site to change your choice. You can also clear the saved preference through your browser’s site-data settings. Google Fonts and third-party contact services may make their own requests when used; the Kioo analytics choice controls only the optional Vercel Web Analytics script.

Changes and contact

If we add advertising pixels, cookie-based analytics, embedded media or other technologies that materially change this position, this notice should be updated and the site’s controls reviewed before launch. Questions about this notice can be sent to hello.kioostudio@gmail.com.

This page is an initial transparency notice, not legal advice or a guarantee that every browser or third-party service behaves identically. We should periodically test the deployed website in a clean browser session to confirm actual storage and network behaviour.
