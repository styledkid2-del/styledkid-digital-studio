# Kioo Studio social post sample

`kioo-content-kit-post.png` and `kioo-content-kit-post.webp` are a source-controlled Kioo Studio social creative promoting The Content Kit. The image uses a real photograph by Alena Shekhovtcova from Pexels, listed as free under the Pexels License: https://www.pexels.com/photo/a-woman-in-a-phone-call-8067771/.

The graphic illustrates Kioo's own promotion. The Content Kit service includes post ideas, captions and content direction; the package page lists its scope and exclusions.
