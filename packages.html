DESIGNED TO WELCOME MORE PEOPLE

Clearer experiences, considered from the start.

We can discuss who you want your work to reach and make practical choices that help more people use it. Depending on the project, our accessibility-informed checks consider:

  • Readable text and clear colour contrast
  • Usability on mobile screens
  • Useful descriptions for meaningful images
  • Clear labels and instructions for forms
  • Keyboard navigation and visible focus
  • Lightweight pages that are mindful of data and connection limits

These are accessibility-informed checks, not a formal accessibility audit or a claim of WCAG compliance. The checks and deliverables included will be agreed for each project.
