GUIDE 01 / PREPARATION

Get ready for your first website

Before choosing colours or layouts, write down what your business does, who it serves and what visitors should do next.

  • One clear sentence describing your offer
  • Your services or product categories
  • Phone, email, location and opening hours, where relevant
  • Real photos and written permission to use supplied assets
  • A clear way for customers to enquire or buy

GUIDE 02 / DOMAIN BASICS

Choose a domain you can control

A domain is the address people type to reach your website. Choose a name that is easy to say, spell and connect to your business.

  • Check spelling, readability and brand fit
  • Compare renewal prices, not only first-year offers
  • Register it using an email account you control
  • Keep renewal reminders and recovery details up to date
  • Give collaborators only the access they need

Before paying, check the registrar’s current terms, renewal cost and available domain extension.

GUIDE 03 / BUDGET

Understand what a website quote covers

Two website quotes can look different because the deliverables may be different. Ask for the scope in writing before comparing prices.

  • Number of pages and what each page includes
  • Mobile layout, contact forms and integrations
  • Who provides copy, images and product information
  • Domain, hosting, paid tools and renewal costs
  • Revision rounds, timeline and payment milestones
  • What support is available after launch

GUIDE 04 / TRUST

Make it easy to trust your business online

Customers should be able to understand who you are, what you offer and how to contact you without having to guess.

  • Use consistent business details across channels
  • Show genuine products, services and examples
  • Explain pricing or how customers can request it
  • Make contact details easy to find on mobile
  • Publish privacy information that matches what your site actually does

Never publish invented reviews, client logos or performance claims. Ask permission before sharing a client project.
