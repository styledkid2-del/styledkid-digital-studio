CLEAR SCOPE BEFORE WORK

Know what is included.
No surprise promises.

Every website and hosting setup is different. Before accepting support work, we confirm what can be accessed, what needs changing, the fee and the expected timing.

01 / TELL US THE ISSUE

Share the website and the change

Send the live URL, describe what you want changed, and tell us who manages the website, domain and hosting.

02 / REVIEW THE SETUP

Check access and dependencies

We identify whether the request depends on the site platform, hosting provider, third-party services, licences or access that you control.

03 / AGREE THE WORK

Confirm the quote and timing

Before work begins, we agree the deliverables, price, schedule, revision expectations and any third-party costs in writing.

04 / HAND OVER CLEARLY

Know what was changed

For agreed work, we explain the changes made and note any follow-up actions or items that remain outside the agreed scope.

IMPORTANT TO KNOW

Support should fit
your actual setup.

This page describes examples of work that may be scoped; it is not a promise of a fixed maintenance plan or 24/7 emergency service. Backups, security monitoring, software updates, uptime guarantees and hosting management are only included if they are specifically checked, available and agreed in writing. Domain, hosting, paid plugin and third-party service fees may be separate.
